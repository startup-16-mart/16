About Us

Discover the idea behind SIXTEEN MART.

SIXTEEN MART is an online lifestyle store created by a group of 16 university students with the idea of bringing useful, affordable and trendy products to Sri Lankan online shoppers.

The name "16 Mart" represents the 16 members who started the business together. Each member contributes ideas and effort towards building the brand.

We focus on carefully selected home accessories and beauty accessories that are practical, simple and suitable for everyday use.

Our tagline, "Always More Value", reflects our aim to provide useful products at affordable prices while creating a simple and convenient online shopping experience.
